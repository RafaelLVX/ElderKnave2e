# ELDER MAGIC FOR KNAVE

*Optional rules for fantasy-based adventures*

---

# Introduction

This module expands **Knave’s** action gameplay with a familiar yet fresh framework of magic drawn from certain open‑world traditions of spellcraft, where practice and adaptability define mastery. The goal is to bring the thrill of high‑stakes adventure and flexible spellcasting to the table while maintaining Knave’s fast, balanced core.

The system transforms magic into a set of attribute-like **Schools**, each functioning as its own skill. This change introduces variety and tactical decision-making: every caster develops unique magical strengths instead of a single universal INT score. Players may pursue focused specialization within a single School or spread their efforts broadly across several, each approach offering distinct advantages. The result is deep, controlled customization—spellcasting feels dynamic and personal, with a stronger connection between character growth and in-world mastery.

### Balance Philosophy

Elder Knave aims to add **combat utility and expressive play** to Knave’s magic without upsetting its equilibrium of risk and reward. Power is distributed through several balancing levers:

- **Attribute Cost:** Each School is a separate ability, requiring deliberate investment.
- **Dice-based resolution:** Because spells often influence combat directly and occur more often than in standard Knave play, each spell requires a roll, much like an attack.
- **Limited Resource:** Mana is scarce and slow to recover, promoting short, intense bursts of power.
- **Tome Rarity:** The availability of spell tomes limits the spread of higher-tier magic, just as rare weapons or relics shape martial progression in Knave.
- **Tier Unlocks:** Higher tiers represent mastery and specialization rather than exponential power growth.
- **Gritty Survival:** The core pillars of Knave remain. Health is still tied to **CON**, and hit points are few and hard to restore. Healing magic exists but is never abundant, ensuring injury, hunger, and exhaustion remain meaningful threats.
- **Encumbrance and Inventory:** Spell tomes, components, and gear occupy inventory slots, keeping Knave’s item-based economy central to survival and decision-making.

These elements ensure that mages gain exciting tactical options without overwhelming other archetypes. The game retains its hallmark **risk-versus-reward tension**: magic offers power, but time, space, and resource limits preserve the struggle. Magic remains dangerous, rewarding, and precious—an equal part of Knave’s world of perilous exploration and earned triumph.

# Character Creation Changes

These rules expand upon the normal Character Creation rules on Knave page 4.

1. **Abilities:** PCs still have the six standard abilities (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA).  
   In addition, five new **Schools of Magic** (Alteration, Conjuration, Destruction, Illusion, Restoration) are rated 0–10 like other abilities.  
   Points may be placed in these during leveling as normal.  
   They are used for all checks related to that discipline.
2. **Mana:** A PC's total Mana equals their **INT** score plus 1.  
   Mana is spent when casting and returns over time (see Recovering Mana below).
3. **Spellbooks:** Spellbooks no longer have per-day limits.  
   They are reusable tools a caster can draw upon as long as their mental strength allows. Each carried spell tome occupies **one item slot.**
4. **Starting Spells and Tomes:** PCs begin with one **Novice-tier spell tome** for each point invested in a School during character creation.  
   For example, a PC with 2 points in Destruction and 1 point in Illusion begins play with two Destruction tomes and one Illusion tome.

### Tiers of Skill in Magic

| Tier               | School Level | Mana Cost | TN = 11 + Tier | Example Spells                          |
| ------------------ | ------------ | --------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Novice (1)**     | 0–4          | 1         | 12             | Conjure Familiar, Flames, Healing Hands |
| **Apprentice (2)** | 5–6          | 2         | 13             | Charm, Ice Spike, Levitate              |
| **Adept (3)**      | 7–8          | 3         | 14             | Command Spirit, Invisibility, Ironflesh |
| **Master (4)**     | 9–10         | 4         | 15             | Bound Armor, Dominate, Sunburst         |

### Tomes and Spells "Known"

- Spells are found in tomes or taught by mentors, usually masters of a School, who create a tome as part of their instruction.
- A caster may cast any spell for which they possess the tome **and** meet the required School tier.
- Tomes serve as both focus and record—keeping a spell ready for use requires carrying its tome. 
- Each carried tome occupies **one item slot.**

# Spellcasting

These rules replace the normal Spellcasting rules on Knave page 21.

To cast a spell:

1. **One free hand is required** for spell gestures, or for holding a tome.
1. **Spend Mana** equal to the spell’s tier.
2. **Make a check:** d20 + School vs. Target Number (11 + Tier).
3. If total ≥ TN, the spell manifests.  
   If targeting a creature (or several creatures in an area), use the creature's Defense (11 + Armor Points), or 11 + Target's Level for mental effects, if higher than TN. A single roll can beat some but not all creatures in an area (affects only those you beat).
4. Failure only expends Mana.

### Dual Casting

Gesturing a spell with both hands for more focused power will:

- **Double** a spell's duration (or enhance its main effect when specified).
- **Double** its Mana cost.

If not specified in the spell's description, assume double duration or GM's decision.

No weapon or shield in hand is allowed when dual casting.

### Optional Rule: Critical Success (Roll 21+)

On a total of 21 or higher, choose one **Arcane Surge** effect.

| d6 | Effect                                            |
| -- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | Empower: Double duration or range.                |
| 2  | Efficiency: Refund 1 Mana.                        |
| 3  | Overcharge: +1 damage die (or +2 if non-damage).  |
| 4  | Chain: Affects one extra target within 10 ft.     |
| 5  | Control: Ignore armor or environmental penalty.   |
| 6  | Player’s Choice: Repeat or combine minor effects. |

Master-level casters choose two effects instead of one.

Apply this rule only when it feels natural to the scene. Use these effects sparingly—overuse can drift away from Knave’s core simplicity and tone.

### Recovering Mana

- Regain **1 Mana per hour** of calm activity or travel.
- Regain **2 Mana per hour** of sleep.

### Mana Gems

A Mana Gem can hold up to **4 Mana** worth of energy. When held in one or both hands, it may fuel one spell of any tier or School (even for dual casting). The gem then crumbles to dust.

An empty gem must be filled first with **Soul Trap** or a similar ritual. Both empty and filled Mana Gems are rare, and coveted by spellcasters.

### Example Spell

**Flames**  
Novice Destruction (Tier 1, Cost 1 Mana)  
Spray flames from your hands in a 10‑foot line or area, scorching whatever they touch. Requires a Destruction check vs. the target’s Defense (or 12 if none).  
On success, deal **d6 fire damage** to a creature or ignite flammable objects in range.  
Can be cast two‑handed at double cost to deal **d6+2 fire damage** or widen the affected area to hit an additional target.

Normally, spell descriptions are brief; this one is more detailed to illustrate the mechanics clearly.

# Alteration Spells

*Manipulate matter and motion—armor, weight, water, and time itself.*

### Novice (Tier 1)

1. **Oakflesh** – Skin hardens like bark. Gain d4 temporary HP for 30 seconds.
2. **Telekinesis** – Move an object (weighing up to two slots) in the air for 20'. Dual cast for 40' or four slots worth of items.
3. **Waterwalking** – Walk (or run) over water for 1 minute.

### Apprentice (Tier 2)

4. **Levitate** – Float in the air in any direction for 1 minute (move up to 2' per second).
5. **Stoneflesh** – Skin hardens like stone. Gain 2d4 temporary HP for 30 seconds.
6. **Water Breathing** – Breathe underwater for one Turn (10 minutes).

### Adept (Tier 3)

7. **Elemental Edge** – A single weapon you can point to gets imbued in fire, cold or electricity and deals extra +2 damage for 30 seconds.
8. **Feather** – Halve carried weight for two Turns (20 minutes).
9. **Ironflesh** – Skin tough as metal. Gain 3d4 temporary HP for 30 seconds.

### Master (Tier 4)

10. **Mass Levitate** – Up to six creatures float in the air at will for 1 minute (move up to 2' per second).
11. **Paralyze** – One creature becomes immobilized and falls prone for 20 seconds.
12. **Time Dilation** – Slow time for 20 seconds. Act twice for the next two rounds.

# Conjuration Spells

*Call forth bound spirits, weapons, and entities from beyond.*

### Novice (Tier 1)

1. **Banish Lesser Spirit** – Force lesser summoned beings to vanish (up to level 2).
2. **Bound Dagger** – Create a spectral dagger (d6 damage) for 1 minute.
3. **Conjure Familiar** – Summon a minor corporeal spirit for 30 seconds. Obeys simple commands.

### Apprentice (Tier 2)

4. **Bound Blade** – Create a spectral war ax or greatsword (d8 damage) for 1 minute.
5. **Conjure Warrior** – Summon a spectral fighter for 30 seconds. Obeys simple commands.
6. **Soul Trap** – Mark a monster or animal's soul to fill a Mana Gem up to its level, if it dies within 20 seconds.

### Adept (Tier 3)

7. **Bound Bow** – Summon a spectral bow (d8 damage) with full quiver for 1 minute.
8. **Command Spirit** – Take control of one summoned being for 20 seconds. You can order it to vanish, if desired.
9. **Conjure Elemental** – Summon an elemental spirit to fight alongside you for 30 seconds.

### Master (Tier 4)

10. **Bound Armor** – Surround yourself in spectral armor (+3 Armor) for 30 seconds.
11. **Conjure Demon** – Summon a bound infernal warrior to fight alongside you for 30 seconds.
12. **Grand Binding** – Merge with a summoned creature for 30 seconds, extending its duration and gaining its Armor and Damage. You can't use your own equipment and reappear when it dies or time ends.

# Destruction Spells

*Harnessing elemental fury—fire, frost, and lightning unleashed.*

### Novice (Tier 1)

1. **Flames** – Emit a short burst of fire (up to 10' away, d6 fire, ignites flammables). Dual cast for d6+2 or wider area (hit one additional target).
2. **Frostbite** – Chill a target (up to 10' away, d6 cold, halves movement for one round). Dual cast for d6+2 or wider area (hit one additional target).
3. **Spark** – Launch a bolt of electricity (up to 15' away, d6 lightning). Dual cast for d6+2 or wider area (hit one additional target).

### Apprentice (Tier 2)

4. **Firebolt** – Hurl a flaming projectile (up to 50' away, d6+2 fire, ignites flammables). Dual cast for 2d6+2.
5. **Ice Spike** – Shoot a shard of ice (up to 50' away, d6+2 cold, halves movement for one round). Dual cast for 2d6+2.
6. **Shock Orb** – Release a crackling orb (up to 30' away, explodes for d6+1 lightning in 10' diameter). Dual cast for 2d6+1.

### Adept (Tier 3)

7. **Chain Lightning** – Strike one target you can point to (d6+1 lightning) and arc to another within 15' and anyone in between. Dual cast: up to three points arc.
8. **Cone of Cold** – Blast frost in a 30' cone in front of caster (d6+1 cold, halves movement). Dual cast for 2d6+1.
9. **Fireball** – Detonate fire (d6+1 fire, ignites flammables) in a 20' diameter area you can point to. Dual cast for 2d6+1.

### Master (Tier 4)

10. **Blizzard** – Freeze a 30' diameter area you can point to (d6+2 cold, halts movement for 2 rounds). Dual cast for 2d6+2.
11. **Inferno** – Summon rolling waves of flame centered up to 30' away from you (30' diameter, d6+2 fire, ignites flammables) for 3 rounds.
12. **Tempest** – Unleash a lightning storm in 30' diameter area you can point to (d6+4 lightning) for 3 rounds.

# Illusion Spells

*Bend emotion, perception, and presence.*

### Novice (Tier 1)

1. **Calm** – One creature stops fighting for 20 seconds unless harmed.
2. **Candlelight** – Create a floating orb of light that follows you for 10 minutes.
3. **Frighten** – One creature flees (or cowers in fear if cornered) for 20 seconds unless harmed.

### Apprentice (Tier 2)

4. **Chameleon** – Your outline blurs for 1 minute. Blend with surroundings while still.
5. **Charm** – One creature regards you as friendly for 1 minute. Ends if you or allies act hostile.
6. **Dread** – Up to two creatures flee (or cower in fear if cornered) for 20 seconds unless harmed.

### Adept (Tier 3)

7. **Frenzy** – One creature attacks the nearest being for 20 seconds.
8. **Invisibility** – You become invisible for 20 seconds.
9. **Phantasmal Image** – Create a convincing illusory figure or creature for 30 seconds. It makes no sound but can mislead or draw attacks.

### Master (Tier 4)

10. **Dominate** – One creature obeys your spoken commands for 20 seconds. Won't self-harm.
11. **Mass Invisibility** – You and one ally become invisible for 20 seconds. Dual cast: you and two allies.
12. **Mayhem** – All creatures in a 20' diameter area you can point to act violently for 20 seconds, attacking the nearest being.

# Restoration Spells

*Heal, purify, and smite the unholy.*

### Novice (Tier 1)

1. **Cure Ailment** – End one poison, disease, or magical affliction.
2. **Healing Hands** – Touch a creature to restore d6 HP. Dual cast: d6+3.
3. **Sun Fire** – Launch a ray of radiant light up to 20'; deal d6 radiant damage to one undead creature. Dual cast for d6+3.

### Apprentice (Tier 2)

4. **Resist Elements** – Ignore one elemental damage type (fire, cold, or lightning) for 1 minute.
5. **Turn Undead** – Undead creatures within 20' flee or cower for 20 seconds.
6. **Ward of Light** – Create a shining barrier for 30 seconds. Grants +1 Armor and negates the next spell that targets you. Dual cast for +2 Armor.

### Adept (Tier 3)

7. **Absorb Health** – Drain life from a creature within 20', dealing d4+2 damage and restoring that much HP to you. Dual cast for 2d4+2.
8. **Fortify Attribute** – One ally gains +2 in checks or attacks using one chosen attribute for 1 minute.
9. **Mend Flesh** – Touch a creature to restore 1 Wound. Dual cast for 2 Wounds.

### Master (Tier 4)

10. **Circle of Protection** – A radiant 20' aura lasts 30 seconds; undead and demons in it flee for 20 seconds.
11. **Radiant Recovery** – Instantly heal 1 Wound for all allies within 20'. Dual cast for 2 Wounds.
12. **Sunburst** – Flood a 20' diameter area you can point to with blinding light; undead and demons in it take 2d6+2 radiant damage. Dual cast for 3d6+4.

# Designer Commentary

Elder Magic for Knave was built to merge Knave’s fast, lethal simplicity with the atmosphere and depth of magic from open-world roleplaying videogames. Rules and spells were tuned to feel authentic to a certain logic while remaining faithful to Knave’s concise balance and constant tension between risk and reward. 

The main design goal was to evoke the feel of a living, magical world without compromising Knave’s minimalism. Magic should empower but never trivialize the struggle.

### Why Schools Instead of a Single Casting Stat?

Each School acting as its own ability emphasizes specialization. A mage who invests deeply in Destruction feels distinct from one versed in Alteration. This creates identity and tactical diversity—players can focus narrowly or dabble broadly, both viable approaches. It also prevents characters from dominating all magical fields.

### Healing and Wounds

Knave’s dual HP and Wound system guided Restoration’s design. Only a few spells truly heal Wounds; most restore HP or prevent damage. This keeps injury meaningful and long‑term, reinforcing Knave’s tone of fragile survival.

### Damage and Duration Choices

All numbers remain small. Spell damage and durations scale gently. This ensures magic feels powerful but finite. Two‑handed casting grants a momentary burst of potency at the cost of flexibility.

### Mana and Recovery

Mana is deliberately scarce in comparison to high-powered fantasy games. Regeneration occurs slowly, so casters should plan spells strategically. Mana Gems give optional bursts of power but remain costly and optional, making resource management central to pacing.

### Power versus Power

Powerful magics are not exclusive to player characters—other beings and rival mages wield them as well. Opponents should use spells intelligently, shaping the world’s dangers and its economy. In practice, the best source of rare tomes often lies in the possession of defeated spellcasters, reinforcing the idea that knowledge and power must be earned.

### Faithfulness to Inspiration

While heavily inspired by open‑world fantasy videogames, every spell or mechanic is reinterpreted through Knave’s lens—simpler, faster, and setting‑neutral.

### Optional Use and Integration

Elder Magic works as a plug‑in: drop it into any Knave campaign without altering the core system. Game Masters can treat tomes as treasure, restrict certain Schools, or tie Mana regeneration to in‑world rituals to fit darker or grittier tones.
